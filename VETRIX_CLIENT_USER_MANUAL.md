# Vetrix Mobile App — User Manual

A simple guide for pet owners

Vetrix helps you manage your pets, request clinic visits, view health records, and order clinic products for pickup.

## 1. Before you start

Have these ready:

- Your phone with Vetrix installed or opened using the instructions provided by the clinic.
- An internet connection to load clinic updates and send requests.
- An email address you can open and your phone number.
- Your pet's details and a clear photo when adding a pet.

If you do not have the app yet, ask the clinic how to access it.

In this guide, **tap** means touch a button once. **Scroll** means move your finger up or down to see more of the page. Words in **bold** match buttons or labels in the app.

## 2. Create an account and log in

### First-time users

1. Open Vetrix and tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter your full name, email, phone number, and a password.
3. Make your password at least 10 characters long. Include a capital letter, a small letter, a number, and a symbol, such as `!`.
4. Tap **Send Account Request**.
5. Wait for the clinic to approve your account. After approval, check your email for a six-digit code.
6. Open **Verify approval code** from the login screen if you have left the verification page.
7. Enter your email and the code, then tap **Verify Code**.
8. On the login screen, enter your email and password and tap **Login**.

**What happens next:** Your Home screen opens after a successful login. Sending an account request alone does not give you access; clinic approval and code verification are required.

If the email has not arrived, check your Spam or Junk folder. Contact the clinic to check your approval request if needed.

### Returning users

Enter your email and password, then tap **Login**. Tap the eye beside the password to check what you typed.

## 3. Find your way around

Use the five buttons at the bottom of the screen:

| Button | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | See care reminders, upcoming visits, and useful shortcuts. |
| **Pets** | Add pets, view their profiles, and request changes. |
| **Book** | Request a clinic visit and check appointments. |
| **Records** | Read medical and vaccination records. |
| **More** | Open your profile, settings, pet QR code, products, orders, and notifications. |

Tap the pet picture or paw with a small downward arrow near the top to choose a pet or **All pets**. This changes the information shown on Home and Records, the QR selection, and the starting pet choice for booking.

Tap the bell to read clinic updates. Use the back arrow to return from a details page. On pages with a refresh feature, pull the list downward and release to load the latest information.

## 4. Add your pet

1. Tap **Pets**, then **Add Pet**.
2. Fill in **Pet basics**: pet name, pet type, sex, breed, birthdate, weight in kilograms, and color or markings.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Fill in **Known allergies**, **Emergency notes**, and **Behavior / habits / food**. Write “none” in a field if it does not apply.
5. Tap **Continue**.
6. Tap **Choose photo** and select a clear photo of your pet. A photo is required. Use a JPG, PNG, or WEBP image up to 3 MB.
7. Check the details. Use **Back** if you need to correct anything.
8. Tap **Submit profile**.

**What happens next:** The pet appears as **Waiting for clinic**. You can book visits and use its QR code after the clinic approves the profile.

### Understand your pet's status

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting for clinic** | Your pet's profile is waiting for review. |
| **Clinic visit needed** | Contact the clinic to arrange in-person confirmation. |
| **Approved** | The clinic has accepted the profile. |
| **Rejected** | The profile was not accepted. Contact the clinic for the next step. |

Use the status buttons on the Pets page to find pets in each group. Choose **All** to see every pet.

## 5. View or correct your pet's details

To see a pet's information, tap **Pets**, then **View Profile** beside that pet. Scroll to see its profile, appointments, medical records, vaccinations, and QR information when available.

To correct an approved profile:

1. Tap **Pets** and find the pet.
2. Tap **Request Changes**.
3. Change the details and tap **Continue** through the form.
4. Review your changes and tap **Request changes**.
5. Open **Pet Edit Requests** on the Pets page to check progress. Tap a request to see the changes and any clinic note.

**What happens next:** Your current approved details remain in use while the clinic reviews the request. To change an existing pet photo, contact the clinic.

## 6. Book a clinic visit

Your pet must have an **Approved** profile before booking.

1. Tap **Book**, then **Request Now**. You can also start with **Book a Visit** on Home.
2. Select your pet and a service: Wellness Checkup, Consultation, Vaccination, Grooming, or Emergency Review.
3. Tap **Continue**.
4. Choose your preferred date and time.
5. Tap **Review Request**.
6. Enter the **Reason for visit** and check the pet, service, date, and time.
7. Tap **Send Request**.

**What happens next:** The request appears in **My Appointment Timeline**. A **Pending** request is still waiting for the clinic. Check for **Approved** status and clinic updates before your visit.

For urgent concerns, contact the clinic directly. Choosing Emergency Review still sends a request for clinic review.

### Check or cancel an appointment

Open **Book** and scroll to **My Appointment Timeline**. Use **All**, **Approved**, **Pending**, **Completed**, or **Cancelled** to find a visit.

To cancel a pending or approved appointment, tap **Cancel** on that appointment and confirm. Check that its status changes to **Cancelled**. If you need a different schedule, contact the clinic or cancel and send a new request; the new request also needs approval.

### Rate a completed visit

1. Open **Book** and select **Completed** in **My Appointment Timeline**.
2. Tap **Rate your visit** on the appointment.
3. Choose **1–5 stars** and optionally write a comment.
4. Tap **Submit rating**.

Your saved rating replaces the button. You can rate each completed visit once;
only you and the clinic can see the feedback. If submission fails, your choices
remain in the form so you can try again. Tap **Not now** to close without submitting.

## 7. Read medical and vaccination records

1. Tap **Records**.
2. Choose your pet, or choose **All Pets**.
3. Tap **Medical** to read medical history or **Vaccination** to see vaccine records.
4. Scroll through the entries. For vaccinations, check the next due date and any **Overdue**, **Due soon**, or **Upcoming** label.

The clinic adds these records. If an entry is missing or incorrect, contact the clinic. You cannot edit medical or vaccination records here.

## 8. Show your pet's QR code at the clinic

A QR code is the square pattern that staff can scan to find your pet's record.

1. Tap **More**, then **QR Pet Retrieval**.
2. Select your pet's name.
3. Show the displayed code to clinic staff.

Staff can scan it with a phone camera and sign in to the Vetrix website to view the protected record.

If **QR unavailable** appears, check that your pet is approved. If it is approved and still has no code, ask the clinic to activate one.

## 9. Buy clinic products

### Place an order

1. Tap **More**, then **Clinic Products**.
2. Browse the products or use **Search product or category**.
3. Tap **Add to cart** for an available item. Adjust the quantity if needed.
4. Tap **View cart** or **Review cart**.
5. Check the items, quantities, and total price. Prices are in Philippine pesos.
6. Choose **Pay at the clinic** or **GCash**, if available.
7. Add an optional pickup note.
8. Tap **Place order** beside the total amount.

**What happens next:** Open the order details to check payment and pickup progress. Orders are collected at the clinic; this checkout is for pickup.

### Pay at the clinic

Choose **Pay at the clinic** when ordering. Check for **Ready for pickup**, then collect and pay for your products at the clinic. Keep your order number handy.

### Pay using GCash

1. Open your GCash order's details.
2. Check the clinic account name, account number, and exact amount displayed.
3. Open GCash, make the transfer, and save the payment screenshot and reference number.
4. Return to your order in Vetrix.
5. Enter the **GCash reference number**. It must contain 10 to 20 digits.
6. Tap **Choose payment screenshot**. Select a JPEG or PNG image smaller than 5 MB.
7. Tap **Submit payment proof**.

**What happens next:** Payment shows **Awaiting verification** until staff check the transfer. It becomes **Paid** after staff approve it. Check pickup status separately.

If GCash is unavailable or sample account details appear, choose payment at the clinic. Do not send money to sample details.

### Track or cancel an order

Tap **More**, then **My Orders**, then **View order** beside the order number. Read the status and any staff note.

| Order status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Order placed** | Your order has been recorded. |
| **Ready for pickup** | Your products are ready to collect. |
| **Collected** | The clinic has marked your order as collected. |
| **Cancelled** | The order has been cancelled. |

| Payment status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pay on pickup** | Pay when you collect your products. |
| **Awaiting payment proof** | Submit your GCash reference and screenshot. |
| **Awaiting verification** | Staff are checking your payment proof. |
| **Proof needs attention** | Read the staff note and contact the clinic about the correction needed. |
| **Paid** | Staff have confirmed your payment. |

If **Cancel order** is available, tap it and confirm to cancel. If you already sent money, contact the clinic before cancelling. If proof needs attention and money was deducted, contact the clinic before paying again.

## 10. Read notifications

1. Tap the bell near the top, or open **More**, then **Notifications**.
2. Read the clinic updates and reminders.
3. Tap an update to mark it as read, or tap **Mark all as read**.
4. Tap the close button to return.

Marking a message as read only changes its read status. To check an appointment or order, open **Book** or **My Orders**.

## 11. Use directions and the assistant

### Get directions to the clinic

1. On **Home**, tap **Clinic Directions**.
2. Check your **Starting address**, or select **Use my current location**.
3. Choose a travel mode.
4. Tap **Open suggested route**.
5. Continue in Google Maps. Allow location access there if asked and you want to use your current location.

Your saved profile address may be filled in automatically. Changing the starting address here applies to this trip.

### Ask a general pet-care question

1. On **Home**, tap **Ask Vetrix Assistant**.
2. Choose a suggested question or type in **Describe your pet concern**.
3. Tap the send arrow and read the reply.

The assistant provides general guidance. It is an automated guide, not a live conversation with clinic staff. Contact the clinic for diagnosis, treatment, or urgent concerns.

## 12. Update your account and settings

### Change your contact details

1. Tap **More**, then **Profile**.
2. Update your full name, email, phone, or address.
3. Tap **Save changes** and wait for the saved message.

### Change preferences

Tap **More**, then **Settings**. Use the switches for:

- **Night Mode** — use darker screen colors.
- **Clinic Notifications** — change the clinic notification preference.
- **Pet Reminders** — change the pet reminder preference.

Check the app regularly for updates to your appointments and orders.

### Log out

Tap **More**, then **Settings**, then **Log out**. Confirm with **Logout**. You can also log out from **Profile**.

## 13. Reset a forgotten password

OTP means a one-time code sent to your email.

1. On the login screen, tap **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your account email and tap **Request admin approval**.
3. Wait for the clinic to approve the request and email a six-digit reset code.
4. Enter the code in **6-digit reset OTP**.
5. Enter your new password, then type it again in **Confirm new password**. Use the same password rules listed in section 2.
6. Tap **Verify OTP & reset password**.
7. Log in with your new password.

If you left the page, reopen **Forgot password?**, enter your email, and tap **Already approved? Enter your OTP**.

The reset code expires 10 minutes after clinic approval. If it expires, use **Request a new OTP approval** when available. The clinic must approve the new request too.

## 14. Quick help

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| I cannot log in. | Check your email and password. For a new account, complete clinic approval and code verification first. |
| No email code arrived. | Check Spam or Junk and confirm the email you entered. Ask the clinic about your approval request. |
| I cannot submit a form. | Scroll through it and correct any message below a field. New pet profiles require a photo and completed care notes. |
| My pet is missing from booking. | Open **Pets** and check that its status is **Approved**. |
| My records look empty. | Select **All Pets**, check both record types, and refresh. Ask the clinic if records have been added. |
| My pet changes have not appeared. | Open **Pet Edit Requests**. The clinic must review changes before they replace approved details. |
| A photo will not upload. | Check the size and image type: pet photos up to 3 MB; payment screenshots smaller than 5 MB. Choose another image if needed. |
| I cannot submit payment proof. | Enter a 10–20 digit GCash reference and select a payment screenshot. |
| An item is unavailable. | Reduce its quantity or remove it from the cart, then check again. |
| I am unsure whether my order went through. | Open **My Orders** to check. If offered, use **Retry order confirmation** to confirm the same order. |
| Updates will not load. | Check your internet connection. Pull down to refresh where available, or use the retry button shown. Contact the clinic if the problem continues. |

When asking the clinic for help, give your pet's name or order number and the message shown on the screen.
